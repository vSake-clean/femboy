
**Femboy Skins** is a purely client-side Fabric mod: only *you* see the changes, so it works on any
server without installing anything there.

- **~450 built-in skins**, ~150 femboy, ~150 e-girl and ~150 **catboy** skins (popular community
  skins from laby.net), plus a procedural skin generator. Every player gets a skin based on their UUID.
- **3D accessories in 11 slots**, cat/fox/wolf/puppy/bunny/bear/mouse ears, cat/fox/wolf/puppy/bunny/devil
  tails, collars & chokers (bell, three bells, tag, name tag with the player's nick, heart, bow, lock, spikes), a scarf that waves in the wind, halo, devil horns, tiara, flower,
  glasses, face mask, angel/bat/butterfly **wings that flap**, arm warmers, paws and a pleated skirt. Bells jingle as you walk.
  Ears twitch, tails sway and wag, wings flap faster when you run.
- **Reactions & fun**, ears flatten and the tail puffs up on a hiss or when hurt, ears perk up on a meow, the tail
  wags on a bark; hearts and a looping purr while being patted; emotes above the head; an opt-in clicker that turns
  listeners towards you; a two-player leash. Every keybind can be set in the mod's own window.
- **Together**, nose boop, hugs, holding hands, and (only when both agree) carrying in arms or piggyback.
- **Cat body animations** with a hold-to-pick **animation wheel**, sit, knead, loaf, belly-up, nap, beg, dance, stretch, wave, groom and more; automatic
  loaf when idling crouched, stretch after sleeping, blinking, tail twitching while sneaking.
- **Toys**, a laser pointer cats follow with their heads (walking after it is opt-in), a yarn ball you can kick,
  a mouse or feather on a rod. Paw prints on snow and sand, a pat counter on the name tag, and a wardrobe of saved outfits.
- **10 ready kits**, catboy, puppy, fox, wolf, bunny, femboy, bear, devil, angel, fairy.
- **Appearance GUI (press G)**, pick one player, several, yourself or *everyone*, see a live 3D preview,
  and change style, a specific skin, every accessory slot, kits and colours with a click.
- **Pat-pat**, hold R while looking at a player or mob: a petpet hand pats their head, the head squishes
  and a squeaky toy squeaks.
- Everything is also available as `/femboy …` commands.
- The mod window is in English, or in Polish when the game language is Polish.
